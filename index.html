Questions

Before you ask…

Is it really no gym?

Yes — really. Every session is designed for your parlour or bedroom floor space, using your body and everyday household items. The full exercise library is in Chapter 8, and Chapter 12 covers training with no equipment and no power.

I get home at 10pm — will it fit?

That’s exactly who it was built for. The 20-minute Home Strength session is the whole workout, the 5-minute Arrival Reset starts the moment you walk in, and Chapter 10 covers late nights directly — including the Worst-Day Cards for the evenings when even 20 minutes is too much.

I haven’t exercised in years. Can I start?

Yes — Phase 1 (UnSit) assumes a standing start. Weeks 1–2 are about waking the body up and learning the movements gently before anything progresses. You measure your own starting point in Chapter 2 and build from there, at your pace. If a clinician has told you not to exercise, follow their advice first.

Do I have to stop eating rice?

No. Nothing is banned. The Nigerian Plate Rule in Chapter 9 gives your rice, swallow and other staples a portion guide — your fist, your palm, half a plate — so the food stays and the portions get a rule. Suya, beer and owambe nights get their own honest chapter too (Chapter 10).

What if NEPA takes light?

Chapter 12 is literally titled “No Gym, No Power, No Problem.” The sessions need no machines and no screens — once you know your workout for the day, a phone torch and your floor space are enough.
